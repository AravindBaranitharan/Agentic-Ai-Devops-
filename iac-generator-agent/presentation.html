01 · Problem statement

Infra-as-code is slow to write — and dangerous to apply

  • Writing correct Terraform needs specialist knowledge — most people can't.
  • Hand-written HCL is error-prone: wrong syntax, insecure defaults, drift.
  • A single wrong terraform apply can break prod or open a security hole (public bucket, open port).
  • Reviews are a bottleneck — plans are hard to read and slow to sign off.
The gap: teams want the speed of "just describe it" without giving up review, security, and a human approval before anything changes.

02 · Proposed solution

Describe it in English → reviewed Terraform

An IaC Generator Agent: you type what you want in plain language; the agent writes secure Terraform, runs a real terraform plan, explains the diff, checks it against policy — and applies only after a human approves.

01
Generate

gpt-4o writes secure-by-default HCL (AWS v5).

02
Plan

Real terraform plan — see exactly what changes. Nothing applied.

03
Explain

The agent reviews the diff in plain English + risks.

04
Govern

Policy check + human-gated apply.

The agent proposes; a human disposes. The LLM never touches your cloud — Terraform does, and only the read-only plan runs on its own.
